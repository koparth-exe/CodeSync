### 5. Retry Persistence Audit

- **`attempts`**: Enqueued at 0; incremented exclusively on execution failure in `drainQueue()` (L537, L578); persisted in `browser.storage.local` under `codesync:queue:metadata`. Survives worker reconstruction without resetting or double-incrementing.
- **`nextRetryAt`**: Calculated upon failure from `Date.now() + Math.max(backoffDelay, rateLimitDelay)`; persisted as an absolute epoch timestamp in `codesync:queue:metadata`. Read by `drainQueue()` (L454). Remains authoritative across worker restarts.
- **`lastError`**: Durably captures `{ code, message, timestamp }` from `SyncResult.error`. Persisted under fencing authority.
- **`state`**: Strictly typed `QueueState` (`pending`, `processing`, `completed`, `failed`, `requires_attention`, `skipped`). Persisted in `codesync:queue:metadata`.
- **`crashCount` Separation**: Interrupted `PROCESSING` items increment `crashCount` in `reconcileInterruptedItems()` (L380), leaving `attempts` untouched. Execution failures increment `attempts`, leaving `crashCount` untouched.
- **Quarantine**: Terminal quarantine triggered when `attempts >= 5` or `crashCount >= 3` or unrecoverable payload/auth/path failures occur. Quarantined items are ignored by subsequent drain passes.

### 6. Backoff Audit

- **Constants**: `BASE_DELAY_MS = 2000`, `MAX_DELAY_MS = 120_000`, `BACKOFF_FACTOR = 2`, `JITTER_FRACTION = 0.2` ([`src/shared/queue/backoff.ts:1-4`](file:///d:/Parth/Projects/CodeSync/src/shared/queue/backoff.ts#L1-L4)).
- **Progression**:
  - Attempt 1: $2000 \times 2^0 = 2000\,\text{ms} \pm 400\,\text{ms}$ (1.6s – 2.4s).
  - Attempt 2: $2000 \times 2^1 = 4000\,\text{ms} \pm 800\,\text{ms}$ (3.2s – 4.8s).
  - Attempt 3: $2000 \times 2^2 = 8000\,\text{ms} \pm 1600\,\text{ms}$ (6.4s – 9.6s).
  - Attempt 4: $2000 \times 2^3 = 16000\,\text{ms} \pm 3200\,\text{ms}$ (12.8s – 19.2s).
  - Attempt 5: $\ge \text{MAX\_ATTEMPTS} \rightarrow$ `REQUIRES_ATTENTION`.
- **Eligibility & Restart**: Backoff is computed once at failure time and committed to durable storage. Worker reconstruction does not recalculate or reset the backoff deadline.

### 7. Rate-Limit Audit

#### Primary Rate Limit
- **Conditions**: `HTTP 429` OR (`HTTP 403` with `x-ratelimit-remaining === 0`).
- **Processing**: Header `x-ratelimit-reset` parsed from epoch seconds to milliseconds. Error constructed as `GitHubRateLimitError` (`isSecondary: false`). Bubbles immediately to `QueueManager`, setting `item.nextRetryAt = resetTimestamp`.

#### Secondary Rate Limit
- **Conditions**: `HTTP 403` with `Retry-After` header present and `remaining > 0`.
- **Processing**: Header `retry-after` parsed in seconds. Error constructed as `GitHubRateLimitError` (`isSecondary: true`, `retryAfterSeconds: N`). Strictly differentiated from authorization failure (`GITHUB_FORBIDDEN`).

#### Effective Delay Dominance
- **Processing**: `QueueManager` evaluates $\max(\text{backoffDelay}, \text{rateLimitDelay})$. Whichever delay is larger governs `item.nextRetryAt`. The resulting timestamp is persisted to `browser.storage.local`.

### 8. Fencing / Lease Audit

- **Mechanism**: Two-tier concurrency control via Web Locks (`codesync_queue_drain`) and durable persistent lease (`codesync:queue:lease`) with monotonic `fencingToken` ([`src/shared/queue/concurrency.ts`](file:///d:/Parth/Projects/CodeSync/src/shared/queue/concurrency.ts)).
- **Stale Worker Prevention**: Every state mutation in `QueueManager` validates `validateFencingToken(workerId, fencingToken)` immediately prior to committing updates.
- **Evidence**: Verified in `FENCE-INT-03` and `CRASH-INT-05`. If Worker A's lease expires while suspended, and Worker B acquires fence $N+1$, Worker A's subsequent mutation attempt throws `StaleLeaseError` and is discarded. Stale workers cannot overwrite retry state or schedule obsolete retries.

### 9. Alarm / Startup Audit

- **Registration**: Periodic alarm `codesync_queue_drain_alarm` registered in `src/entrypoints/background.ts` with period `QUEUE_DRAIN_ALARM_PERIOD_MINUTES = 5`.
- **Eligibility Gate**: `QueueDrainer.drain("alarm")` and `drain("startup")` delegate to `QueueManager.drainQueue()`. Line 454 checks `(item.nextRetryAt ?? 0) <= now`. If `now < nextRetryAt`, the item is skipped.
- **Evidence**: Verified in `ALARM-06`, `CRASH-INT-04`, `RETRY-REC-03`, `RATE-REC-01`, and `RATE-REC-02`. Neither periodic alarms nor startup events can bypass persisted retry delays.

### 10. WAL / Recovery Interaction

- **Protocol**: Intent-first WAL in IndexedDB (`wal_logs`).
- **Interrupted PROCESSING Recovery**: Crashed items in `PROCESSING` have `crashCount` incremented in `reconcileInterruptedItems()`. Non-crashed items in `FAILED` with future `nextRetryAt` are left untouched in `FAILED` state.
- **Orphan Reconciliation**: Orphan payloads recovered to `REQUIRES_ATTENTION`. Orphan metadata quarantined to `REQUIRES_ATTENTION`. All recovery mutations are guarded by fencing token validation.
- **Idempotency**: Multiple recovery passes leave durable retry state unchanged.
